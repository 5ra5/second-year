Two versions we use: ISO 25010, Garvin's 8 dimensions of product quality management


- Quality Concepts - name and reasoning
- Measurable Quantities - metric
- Counts taken from Design Representations - how we measure the metric

examples below are likely to appear in the final submission of the SRS document
example:
- Quality Concepts - Reliability
- Measurable Quantities - Mean time to failure?
- Counts taken from Design Representations - Run it and count crashes per hour

example:
- Quality Concepts - Complexity
- Measurable Quantities  - Information flow between modules?
- Counts taken from Design Representations - Count procedure calls

example:
- Quality Concepts - Usability
- Measurable Quantities  - Time to learn and use?
- Counts taken from Design Representations - Minutes taken for some user task
