Q1. What does selection (σ) do in relational algebra?
-  It chooses rows from a relation that satisfy a given condition

Q2. Relation R(A, B) has 3 tuples and relation S(C, D, E) has 5 tuples. What are the **degree and cardinality** of R × S?
-  Degree = 5, Cardinality = 15

Q3. In the **relational model**, what is a **tuple**?
-  A single row representing one record in a table

Q4. Which relational algebra expression is **equivalent** to σA>10(σB=′X′(R)) ?
-  σA>10∧B=′X′(R)

Q5. A relation COURSE(CID, Title, Credits, Dept) has candidate keys {CID} and {Title, Dept}. Which is a **superkey but not candidate key**?
-  {CID, Title}
