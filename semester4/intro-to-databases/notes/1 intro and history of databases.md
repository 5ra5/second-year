## Evolution of databases

**Database** = a collection of related data. It is used to extract information and get something out of it. For example, data analysts look at data and present relevant information to the people that need it to do a certain job.

It would be very difficult to operate with data if it was only stored on hard drives when it comes to complex and large data.

-  1950s - 1960s: File systems
-  1960s - 1970s: Hierarchical & Network models (tree & graph structures)
-  1970s onward: Relational Model
-  2000s - Today: NoSQL & Diversification
