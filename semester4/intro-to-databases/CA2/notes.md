you will mostly use binary relationships, sometimes ternary (there is a way to simplify from ternary to binary)
## entities and their properties

-  at least 5 entities
-  between 3 and 5 **simple attributes** for each entity
-  2 of the entities should have **complex attributes**
-  1 entity should have **multi-valued attribute**
-  1 of the entities should have a **derived attribute**

-  1 entity should be **weak**

## relationships

an example of each:
-  one-to-one
-  one-to-many
-  many-to-many
-  any other relationship
