# Vision statement

-  System vision - vision of all active stakeholders
-  It is important for communicating our ideas to other people (stakeholders)
-  Not a scientific diagram, it is used to get a good idea of the system
-  A vision statement method - "rich picture"
-  Include only important concepts, not technical detail
-  The diagram should make the system understandable in a few seconds
-  A good practice would be to ask someone that hasn't seen the picture yet to tell you what the system is about
-  This allows us to determine whether the rich picture we created is good or not
-  It is useful to get feedback from stakeholders so we can relate to their goal models
-  We need to identify the core of the system - that's going to be in the centre of the diagram
-  Major features and business/operational contexts would be around the centre, connected by lines depending on their relationship
-  Rich picture informs other RE processes/elements

Rich Picture Challenges:
-  Scoping
-  Completeness
-  Self-explanatory
